Pool Guard

Put USDC into an Aero pool. The guard keeps it staked, sends you the AERO, and takes you out when the pool stops beating simply holding.

Put money in

  • Pay with USDC or WETH. The guard opens in the pool of this pair that has paid most net of price swings (it measures them hourly) and swaps half of what you pay into the other coin there. Range: current price ± 5 steps. Stays staked, so it earns AERO.
  • Reviewed once a day: AERO goes to your wallet, then the position is compared with just holding what you put in.
  • Closed for you if it falls more than 3% behind holding, or sits out of range for 3 hours.
  • Also closed when the pool stops paying: its AERO is worth less than what price swings cost by more than 0.10% a day (measured after 12 hours).
  • On every close the guard tries to turn everything into USDC, so a falling coin is not held after the exit: the other coin is swapped in the same pool, at most 2% below the 30-minute average price. If that swap cannot be done at that moment, you get both coins as they came out.
  • When the guard closes it by itself, the coins wait on the guard for you and go back into the pool that pays once one earns over 0.10% a day net of swings, at two daily reviews 20 hours apart.
  • You can close it, take the position back as it is, or take parked coins back at any time. Nobody else can move them.
