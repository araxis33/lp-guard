Pool Guard

Put USDC into an Aero pool. The guard keeps it staked, sends you the AERO, and takes you out when the pool stops beating simply holding.

Put money in

  • Range: current price ± 5 steps of the pool. Stays staked, so it earns AERO.
  • Reviewed once a day: AERO goes to your wallet, then the position is compared with just holding what you put in.
  • Closed for you if it falls more than 3% behind holding, or sits out of range for 3 hours.
  • Also closed when the pool stops paying: its AERO is worth less than what price swings cost by more than 0.10% a day (measured after 12 hours).
  • You can close or take it back yourself at any time. Nobody else can move it.
